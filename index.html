How It Works

The science behind the difference.

Redox signalling is a normal part of cellular biology. This guide separates that general science from evidence about specific ASEA finished products.

01

Redox signalling — your body's native language

Cells use oxidation-reduction reactions as part of normal signalling and metabolism. General redox biology does not, by itself, establish that a particular supplement or topical product produces a health benefit.

02

ASEA's patented breakthrough

ASEA uses a patented manufacturing process and describes its products through the language of redox signalling. A patent establishes an invention, not clinical effectiveness, so product benefits should be evaluated using appropriate finished-product studies.

03

Evaluate product-specific evidence

Before purchasing, review the current ingredient list, intended-use instructions and studies conducted on the exact finished product. Testimonials describe individual experiences and do not guarantee results.
