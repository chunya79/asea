How this site approaches product information

Useful first

Each page should help you make a decision, not simply repeat a product brochure.

Clear comparisons

Ingestible supplements, topical gels and facial routines are explained as different formats with different purposes.

Honest boundaries

Cosmetic, supplement and medical claims are kept separate, and individual experiences are not presented as guarantees.

Current sources

Prices, ingredients, warnings and availability are checked in the official shop for the customer's market.

Relevant evidence

General redox biology is distinguished from research that directly tests a finished product.

Personal help

Renee can help with product navigation and ordering questions while referring health decisions to qualified professionals.
