Using a New Topical

Three useful checks

Read the supplied directions

Follow the label for application amount and frequency. Do not rely on old social posts or instructions for another market.

Patch test first

When introducing a new topical product, test a small area and stop use if irritation occurs.

Judge the product itself

Look for substantiation involving the finished formula when assessing specific appearance claims.

Renee's Practical Guide

How RENU28 differs from the other options

RENU28 makes most sense when you want a straightforward body-skin gel. REDOXGold has different positioning around a cooling sensation, and RENUAdvanced is a multi-product facial routine.

Keep the routine simple

Apply it only as directed and avoid changing several skin products at once. A simple routine makes it easier to notice how your skin responds.

Give comparisons context

If you track appearance, use the same lighting, camera distance and time of day. Uncontrolled before-and-after photos can exaggerate differences.

Know when to stop

Stop if irritation develops. Persistent or concerning skin symptoms deserve assessment from a qualified clinician.
