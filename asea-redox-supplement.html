Before You Choose

What to check on the current product label

FormatA liquid dietary supplement rather than a tablet, powder or medicine.
IngredientsConfirm the current ingredient list in the official shop for your country.
DirectionsUse only as directed on the supplied label; market instructions can change.
SuitabilityAsk a qualified health professional if you are pregnant, nursing, managing a condition or using medication.
Evidence distinctionRedox reactions are fundamental to human biology. That fact does not by itself prove a health outcome from drinking a particular product. The most relevant evidence is research that tests the finished ASEA REDOX product in people using an appropriate comparison group.
Practical Guide

A sensible way to evaluate it

1. Read the label

Check ingredients, serving directions, storage advice and warnings on the product supplied in your market.

2. Review relevant research

Look for studies of the exact finished product, not only papers explaining redox processes in cells.

3. Set realistic expectations

Supplements are not medicines. Individual testimonials cannot predict your experience and do not replace clinical evidence.

Renee's Buying Guide

Is the drink the right place to start?

I suggest starting with the format you can use consistently. The drink is the ingestible option in this range; RENU28 and REDOXGold are topical products, while RENUAdvanced is a facial skin care routine. They are not interchangeable.

Choose the drink when

You specifically want to consider the liquid supplement and are comfortable following its daily label directions.

Choose a topical when

Your goal is skin care or a topical cooling product rather than an ingestible supplement.

Ask before ordering

If you are unsure about suitability, medication or a health condition, speak with an appropriate health professional first. I can help with product and ordering questions, but not diagnose or prescribe.
