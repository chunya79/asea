Build the Routine

Check the purpose of each step

CleanseRemove surface oil, makeup and daily residue using the current cleanser directions.
TreatApply targeted products in the order and amount stated on their labels.
MoisturiseSupport hydration and skin feel with the appropriate day or night step.
ProtectFollow the supplied daytime directions and use suitable sun protection as needed.
How to read appearance claimsTerms such as “reduces the appearance of fine lines” describe a cosmetic outcome; they do not mean the product changes ageing itself. Look for studies of the finished formula, the number and type of participants, comparison conditions and the length of follow-up.
Before Starting

Make the routine suit your skin

Check every formula

Review ingredients for sensitivities and confirm which products are included in the current set.

Introduce gradually

Patch test new products and consider adding one step at a time so it is easier to identify irritation.

Track realistic changes

Use consistent lighting and timing if you compare appearance. Individual photos and testimonials are not controlled evidence.

Renee's Routine Guide

Decide whether you want a system or one simple step

RENUAdvanced is for someone who wants a coordinated facial routine. If you prefer one topical product, compare RENU28 instead. More steps are not automatically better; consistency and skin tolerance matter.

Morning

Use the supplied order for cleansing, targeted products and daytime moisturising. Add appropriate sun protection rather than assuming a routine replaces it.

Evening

Remove makeup and sunscreen thoroughly, then follow the current night-time sequence without adding every new product at once.

Sensitive skin

Patch test and introduce products gradually. Stop and seek appropriate advice if irritation persists.

Check what is included in the current system and consider your existing routine, preferences and tolerance before buying.

How quickly should appearance change?

Avoid promises based on testimonials. Cosmetic results vary, and meaningful comparisons require consistent use and consistent photography.
